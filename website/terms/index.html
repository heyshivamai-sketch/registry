Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. What Regiday provides

Regiday is a personal, local-first organization tool for tracking document expiry dates, renewal plans, and recurring subscription schedules on your device.

2. Tracking only — no provider actions

Regiday does not connect to banks, process payments, renew official documents, or cancel subscriptions on your behalf. Marking a subscription as cancelled or toggling auto-renew inside Regiday updates your local tracking only and does not change your agreement with any third-party service provider.

3. Reminders and accuracy

Local notifications are scheduled through your device's operating system. Delivery timing depends on device settings, battery optimization, notification permissions, and OS pending-notification limits (such as 64 pending local notifications on iOS). You remain responsible for verifying critical renewal, legal, travel, and payment deadlines directly with issuing authorities and providers.

4. Optional AI scanning and CSV/Gmail suggestions

Fields extracted via optional AI document scanning and recurring charge suggestions from CSV imports or Gmail scans are provided for convenience and may contain errors. You must review and verify all extracted values before saving them.

5. Local data and backups

Because Regiday does not maintain a cloud copy of your records, uninstalling the app, clearing app storage, or losing your device without an encrypted .rgry backup (and its password) will result in permanent loss of your locally stored records.

6. Free tier and Regiday Pro

The Free plan supports up to 10 documents and 5 subscriptions, while existing records always remain viewable, editable, and exportable. Where enabled, Regiday Pro is a non-consumable one-time store purchase tied to your Google Play or Apple account; it does not add cloud sync or transfer between Android and iOS.
