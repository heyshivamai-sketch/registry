Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Local-first by design

Regiday is built as a local-first mobile application. There is no Regiday user account, no cloud document database, and no background sync of your personal records to Regiday servers.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Documents & subscriptions: Stored locally in an on-device SQLite database inside the app's private storage, including names, dates, renewal history, custom fields, and reminder preferences.
  • Document photos & attachments: Stored in on-device app storage and referenced by your local database.
  • Preferences & lock state: App lock preference, onboarding state, and local store entitlement status are stored in device preferences.

3. Optional network features

  • AI Document Scanning (optional, user-initiated): When Firebase AI Scan is configured and you explicitly tap Scan all images, the selected document images are sent to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic (protected by Firebase App Check) solely to extract fields for your review. Choosing or attaching an image does not send it automatically, and extracted fields are never saved without your confirmation.
  • App Store Billing (Regiday Pro, when enabled): In builds where one-time purchases are enabled, Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit handles purchase and restore checks. No registry records are sent to the store.
  • Advertising (when enabled in free builds): In ads-off QA builds, the Mobile Ads SDK is not started and no advertising ID permission is declared. In future free builds with ads enabled, Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent choices apply; no registry record content is shared with ad networks.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used only when you choose to attach or scan a document image.
  • Notifications: Used to schedule local on-device reminders for upcoming expiry, renewal, and payment dates.
  • Biometrics / Device Passcode: Used only if you enable App Lock to hide records behind your device authenticator. App Lock hides records in the UI and app switcher; it does not encrypt the local SQLite database.

5. Encrypted backups (.rgry)

You may export an AES-256 password-encrypted .rgry backup file to a location you choose. Regiday does not store your backup password and cannot recover a lost backup file or password.
