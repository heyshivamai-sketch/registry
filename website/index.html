How it works

Start in under a minute—no account needed.

1

Add or scan your record

Use the guided form to enter a document or subscription manually, or optionally scan photos with Gemini AI and review every field before saving.

2

Pick your lead time

Choose when you want to start renewing or decide before a charge. Regiday schedules local device notifications 30 days, 7 days, 1 day, or on the day itself.

3

Move safely when you change phones

Protect the app with biometrics or passcode, and export a password-encrypted .rgry backup whenever you want an offline copy.

Privacy by architecture

Your information stays on your device.

Regiday has no user account, no cloud document database, and no hidden sync. Everything you save lives in local SQLite storage on your phone.

Local SQLite storage

Documents, renewal history, attachments, and subscriptions are stored directly in your device's app storage.

Optional AI scanning

Document photos are sent to Gemini via Firebase AI Logic only when you explicitly tap Scan all images. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm.

Biometric app lock

Lock Regiday behind Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or device passcode and hide screens in the Android app switcher.

Encrypted .rgry backups

Export an AES-256 password-encrypted archive to Files, Drive, or local storage. Regiday never sees or stores your backup password.

Questions

Straight answers about Regiday.

Do I need an account or cloud sync?

No. Regiday works completely offline without registration. When switching devices, export an encrypted .rgry backup from Profile and import it on your new phone.

What is included in the free plan vs Regiday Pro?

The free plan includes up to 10 documents and 5 subscriptions with reminders, app lock, scanning, and encrypted backups included. Regiday Pro is planned as a one-time upgrade—not a subscription—to remove record count limits on your device.

Does Regiday cancel or charge my subscriptions automatically?

No. Regiday tracks your plans, billing cycles, and decide-by dates locally so you can review or cancel directly with each provider before renewal.

Which languages are supported?

Regiday supports English, French, and Arabic (including native right-to-left layout).
